About us

22 years of keeping Akola writing, printing and working.

Yamuna Enterprises began as a stationery distributor in Akola and, under the leadership of Deepak Chandak, has grown into a supplier that schools, companies and banks rely on every working day. Our distribution network now reaches retailers across more than five districts.

We are authorised distributors for leading stationery brands, and we supply computers and IT peripherals, including iBall products. With more than 2,000 products and a supply chain built over two decades, we can usually fill an order quickly, whether it is a single notebook or a full term's supply for a school.

World of Stationery is our online store. The service and the people stay the same, and now you can order from home.
